
1. Time and Space Complexity

    with recursive:
        recurrence: solve(n) = max(solve(n − 1), num[n] + solve(n − 2))
        time complexity: O(2^n)
        space complexity: O(n) (recursion stack)
    
    with recursive and memoization
        recurrence: dp[n] = max(solve(n - 1), num[n] + solve(n - 2))
        time complexity: O(n)
        space complexity: O(n) (dp array) + O(n) (stack) => O(n)

2. Recursion Tree 
                
        solve(4) = max(solve(3), 5 + solve(2))

        solve(4)
        ├── solve(3)
        │   ├── solve(2)
        │   │   ├── solve(1)
        │   │   └── solve(0)
        │   └── solve(1)
        └── solve(2) 
            ├── solve(1)
            └── solve(0)

3. DP Table 

    | i |  num[i] | max(dp[i - 1], num[i] + dp[i - 2]) | dp[i] |
    | - | ------- |------------------------------------| ----- |

    | 1 |   2     |           max(1, 2 + 0)            |  2    |
